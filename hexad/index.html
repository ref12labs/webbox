Pair this device

  1. On the computer running hexad, open Settings, Remote and choose Pair a device.
  2. Scan the code with this device's camera and open the link.
  3. On an iPhone or iPad: tap Share, then Add to Home Screen, and open hexad from the home screen. iOS only delivers notifications to installed web apps.
